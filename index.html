A letter I couldn't say

You said…
“Jao.”

So I thought maybe this time…
I should actually listen.


But before I go…
there are some things I need you to know.

Chapter 01

I don't know when it happened…

Pehle tumse baat karna achha lagta tha…
phir tumse baat kiye bina din adhura lagne laga.

Aur pata hi nahi chala kab…
tum meri favourite person ban gayi.

My favourite memory ♡
Chapter 02

You probably don't know…

tumhara gussa…

tumhara bina reason mujhe irritate karna…

Shayad tumhare liye normal tha.

The little things… ♡
Chapter 03

The truth about today

Aaj jab maine kaha ki main door jaa raha hoon…

Sach kahun?

Main jaana nahi chahta tha.

“Mat jao.”
Chapter 04

But you said… Jao.

Us ek word ne mujhe bahut kuch samjha diya.

Shayad meri jagah tumhari zindagi mein utni nahi hai…
jitni tumhari meri zindagi mein hai.

Aur shayad isi liye…
mujhe tumse door jaana seekhna padega.


No.

Ye jhoot hai.

Some memories refuse to leave.
Chapter 05

Because I still don't want to go.

Main tumse naraz ho sakta hoon.
Tumse hurt ho sakta hoon.
Kabhi kabhi tumse door hone ki baat bhi kar sakta hoon.

Lekin tumse door rehna?

Nahi pata kaise karunga.

Maine tumhe kabhi paaya hi nahi…
phir bhi tumhe khone ka darr hai.

You became a part of me.
Chapter 06

If this is really goodbye…

Toh thank you.

Meri life ka ek aisa hissa banne ke liye…
jise main chaah kar bhi bhool nahi paunga.

Un conversations ke liye.
Un stupid moments ke liye.
Un smiles ke liye.

Aur un saare moments ke liye…
jinke baare mein tum shayad bhool jaogi,
par main nahi.

Thank you for being you.
Before the last page

But there is one thing
I still haven't said.



I don't want a perfect ending.
I don't want you to feel guilty.
I don't want you to cry.

I just wanted you to know the truth.


I never wanted to leave you.


Main bas chahta tha ki ek baar tum mujhe rok lo.

Agar tum nahi rokogi…

toh main chala jaunga.

Lekin agar tum bas ek baar keh do…

“Ruk ja.”

Main kahin nahi jaunga.
